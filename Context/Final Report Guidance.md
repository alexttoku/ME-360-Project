# Final Report Guidance
For your final report, please write in the IEEE style. See this link to find the IEEE template for Word, LaTeX or Overleaf (use this one for Overleaf) and upload a single PDF to Lamaku. Fail to write your report draft in the correct format will receive a penalty of at least 20% of your grade.

Be sure to cite all your sources (again, Wikipedia is NOT a valid source) and do not copy down exact sentences from your sources. If you want to present an idea or an argument from a reference, write it in your own words and cite the reference. Fail to include references will severely reduce your report grade as that might be considered plagiarism. 

Note on AI Usage: you are allowed to use genAI tools to help you brainstorm and understand concepts for your final project with proper declaration as indicated in the syllabus; however, using genAI tools to generate text for your proposal will be considered as plagiarism and will result in a zero in your proposal grade. 

I expect to see the following sections in your final report

## Introduction & Motivation
in this section, you are expected to introduce your theme, and why this is an important problem to solve. 
## Method
in this section, you are expected to explain what numerical methods you plan to use to solve your theme, and how does the method work.
## Result
in this section, you are expected to report your solution and what the engineering implications are.
## Discussion
in this section, you are expected to discuss how well your numerical methods performed, and whether there is any room of improvement.
## Conclusion
in this section, you are expected to summarize your project and indicate where you can make improvement for future work.
## Acknowledgement
in this section, you are expected to describe every members contribution. You do not need to write this part if you are working on a project alone.
For your draft, you do not need to have everything ready, but I do expect to see a somewhat complete report. If you don’t have time to finish a certain section, just write a short sentence similar to “we expect to perform this computation here”. If you don’t have a figure ready but know where you want to place a figure, put a placeholder there as well.

There is no page limit for the final report. As long as you can provide a comprehensive story of your project, the report can be short.
